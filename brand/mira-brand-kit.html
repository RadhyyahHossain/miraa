01

Strategy

A child's health story is written by many people over many years: parents, grandparents, daycare, specialists, the ER at 2am. Mira's job is to gather it calmly and hand it over clearly. The brand should feel like a well kept notebook, never like a hospital or a toy.

Mira is
  • Calm
  • Prepared
  • Continuous
  • Plain spoken
  • Trustworthy
  • Quietly warm
  • Exact about dates and doses
Mira is not
  • Clinical or sterile
  • Cute (no crayons, bears, balloons)
  • Alarmist
  • Hospital blue
  • AI sparkle
  • Hearts, crosses, stethoscopes
  • Gamified
The idea

A record fills in over time.

Each milestone is the same circle, filled a little more, clockwise from 12 o'clock like a clock hand moving forward. Nothing is ever erased. The mark is a picture of accumulation, which is exactly what Mira does for a family.

03

Wordmark

Drawn, not typed. Built from the same circle.

m

Two arches of radius 12 on a shared stem.

i

The dot is a full circle, the last milestone.

r

The shoulder is a quarter arc, read clockwise.

a

A single story bowl: the empty ring, plus a stem.

Always lowercase. Never set the name in a typeface in place of the drawn wordmark. In running text, write it as Mira.

07

Voice

Plain

Write the way a careful parent talks to a nurse. Short sentences, real words.

Calm

Parents open Mira when something is wrong. No exclamation marks, no alarms, no cheerleading.

Specific

Numbers, dates and doses beat adjectives. "101.4 °F since Tuesday night" beats "a high fever".

09

Motion

One movement: fill clockwise.

When something is added, saved or compiled, the circle advances clockwise from 12 o'clock and settles. It eases in and out over 600 ms and holds still between steps. With reduced motion turned on, it simply shows the final state.
